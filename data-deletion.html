MyVidUploader (shown as vidUpload on Facebook) is a personal tool I use to upload my own videos. It does not keep user data on any server, so there is very little to delete, and you can remove all of it yourself.

Remove the app’s access

  • On Facebook, open Settings, then Apps and Websites (also called Business Integrations).
  • Find vidUpload in the list and choose Remove.
  • On TikTok, open Settings and privacy, then Security, then Manage app permissions, and remove MyVidUploader.

What is stored and where

The only data the app holds is the access token issued when I log in, saved in a file on my own device. It is never sent to anyone else.

Delete the stored data

Delete the app’s local token and settings files on the device where it runs. Removing the app’s access in the steps above also makes any saved token stop working.
