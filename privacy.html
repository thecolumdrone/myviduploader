MyVidUploader Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

MyVidUploader is a personal tool I use to upload my own videos to my TikTok account.

What the app accesses

When I log in with TikTok, the app receives an access token that allows it to upload videos to my account. It may also read basic profile information, such as my display name.

How it is used

The token is used only to upload and publish videos I choose to post. I don't sell, share, or disclose this data to anyone.

Storage and removal

The token is stored securely on my own device. Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions.
